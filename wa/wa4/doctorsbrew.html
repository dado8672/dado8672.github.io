Brewed With Real Labuyo Peppers

Whether its to wind down after a long day or to spice up a great party, Doctor's Brew is the perfect drink for any occasion.
A family recipe passed down generations throughout the Philippines, this specialty brew is carefully crafted using real Saling Labuyo peppers,
preserving the unique sweet and sour taste while toning down the spice.

This was a package design product of a fictional beer company, using inspiration from hot suaces
and old time shipping companies, I created bottle and case designs.
